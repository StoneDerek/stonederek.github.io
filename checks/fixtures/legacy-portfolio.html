Hi, I’m Derek.

I’m an Electrical Engineering student at Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology. I have experience in simulation and ML research. I’m looking to get more hands-on experience and exposure to cutting-edge technologies. I’m particularly interested in semiconductors, photonics, and hardware design.
